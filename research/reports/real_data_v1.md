## Setup

- Data: Binance USDT-M perps, 1h bars, 2022-01-01 to 2026-10-01 (data.binance.vision).
- Universe: monthly top 30 by previous-month quote volume, from every perp ever listed; 291 distinct symbols over the period.
- Coverage of universe symbol-hours: price 100%, funding 100%, spot price 90%.
- Portfolio: dollar-neutral rank weights, gross 1.0, rebalanced 8h or 1D.
- Costs: 5 bps fee + 3 bps slippage per unit traded; funding charged on positions.
- Walk-forward: train 365 days, test 91 days, gap 24 bars; OOS period 2023-01-02 to 2026-09-27 (15 test windows).
- Gate: OOS net Sharpe >= 0.5 and positive in >= 3 calendar years.

## Caveats

- Returns are arithmetic sums of per-bar returns at gross 1.0; no leverage or vol targeting.
- Trades fill at the bar close used for the decision (lag=1); add a bar of delay to stress it.
- Delisted coins keep their last price after delisting (positions exit at the next rebalance), so delisting losses after the final traded price are not modelled.
- Walk-forward still selects among a grid we chose after seeing crypto literature; treat a marginal pass as 'needs more evidence', not as proof.
